About C.A.P.

C.A.P. is a private AI inbox assistant for small businesses. It reads your email on your own computer, works out what needs you, and writes a reply in your voice using only facts you've given it. You stay in charge: it never sends, archives or deletes anything without your click.

How it works

  1. Your mailboxC.A.P. connects straight to your email provider. Mail stays unread on the server.
  2. SortA quick pass on each new email: needs a reply, FYI, urgent, likely fraud, holds sensitive numbers.
  3. DraftUrgent mail first. Drafts use your business profile, saved replies and files, and never invent prices or availability.
  4. CheckEvery price, date, link and promise is highlighted: green when your sources back it, amber when you should look.
  5. You sendEdit if you like, then send. C.A.P. learns from your edits and offers to update your facts.

What's inside

Who needs a reply, who hasn't answered you, tasks due, key dates and today's calendar, with the time you've saved.

Inbox

Clients or Policies

Key dates

Federal tax deadlines worked out for every year and moved off weekends and holidays, plus your own state deadlines and client dates, once or every year.

Knowledge

Business profile, saved replies, and files (PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, text up to 25 MB). Ask questions and get answers with their sources.

Security

A protection score, app lock with a PIN, fraud and sensitive-number checks, automatic clean-up of old email text, and an audit log you can download.

Industry starter kits

Pick tax & bookkeeping or insurance to add 14 ready-made saved replies, "never promise" safety rules and a document checklist. Nothing you've written is changed.

Your choice of AI

Run a free model on your own computer with Ollama, so email never leaves it, or use OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, Groq or OpenRouter with your own API key.

What C.A.P. won't do

  • Send, archive or delete email on its own. There is no auto-send.
  • Invent prices, policies, dates or availability that aren't in your information.
  • Send your data to us. There is no C.A.P. account, server, analytics or telemetry.

Updating

On Windows, run the new installer; it replaces the old version. With the portable ZIP, or on macOS and Linux, replace the old CAP folder (or CAP.app) with the new one. Your email history, settings and clients are stored separately, so they carry over.

Removing C.A.P.

On Windows, open Settings → Apps → Installed apps, find C.A.P. Inbox Assistant, and choose Uninstall. With the portable ZIP, or on macOS and Linux, delete the CAP folder or CAP.app. To remove your data too, delete C.A.P.'s data folder:

  • Windows: %LOCALAPPDATA%\CAP (paste it into File Explorer's address bar)
  • macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/CAP
  • Linux: ~/.local/share/CAP

Saved email and AI passwords are in your system's password store (Windows Credential Manager, macOS Keychain or your Linux keyring) under entries named for C.A.P.; delete them there.

If something goes wrong

Windows: "Failed to resolve Python.Runtime.Loader.Initialize"
Windows blocked the files because they came from the internet. Version 2.2.1 and later fix this by themselves. On an older version, delete the extracted CAP folder, right-click the downloaded ZIP, choose Properties, tick Unblock, click OK, and extract it again.
Nothing happens when I open it
Give it a few seconds the first time. On Windows, make sure you extracted the ZIP first and opened CAP.exe from the extracted folder.
"Ollama isn't running"
Open the Ollama app (or run ollama serve), then click Test and use this model again.
Email won't connect
Most providers need an app password, not your normal password, and two-step verification turned on to create one. C.A.P.'s connect screen links to the right page.
Drafts are slow
Local AI depends on your computer. Try a smaller model, choose Settings → Inbox → Write drafts only when I open an email, or use a cloud AI key.

Privacy & security

C.A.P. was built for offices that handle tax returns, policies and Social Security numbers. Here is exactly what happens to your information.

Where your information lives

Everything C.A.P. knows (email text, drafts, clients, tasks, your business details and files) is kept in one database file on your computer, in C.A.P.'s data folder. There is no C.A.P. account and no C.A.P. server. We never receive your email, your clients' details or anything about how you use the app.

What leaves your computer, and only when

Goes toWhatWhen
Your email providerReading, sending, archiving and deleting your mail, over encrypted connectionsWhen C.A.P. checks for mail, and when you click Send, Done or Delete
Nobody (local AI)With Ollama, drafting happens on your computerNever leaves
The AI company you chose (cloud AI)The text needed to sort and draft each email, under your own API key. You can mask SSNs and account numbers first.Only if you choose a cloud AI
Google CalendarReading your events so drafts avoid busy timesOnly if you connect it

C.A.P. has no analytics, telemetry or crash reporting, and it turns off the reporting option in the AI library it uses.

How C.A.P. protects it

  • Private to this computer. C.A.P. only accepts connections from your own computer. Every change needs a secret code that is new each time C.A.P. starts, and other websites can't load or embed it.
  • App lock. A PIN when C.A.P. opens and after you've been away. The PIN is stored only as a salted hash, wrong guesses lock it for a while, and a recovery code gets you back in.
  • Passwords in your system's vault. Email passwords and AI keys go to Windows Credential Manager, the macOS Keychain or your Linux keyring. If that isn't available, C.A.P. says so in Settings.
  • Fraud and impersonation shield. Lookalike sender addresses, fake IRS messages, payment-change requests and phishing links are flagged before you reply.
  • Sensitive-number guard. Emails holding SSNs, account, card or license numbers are marked, you're warned before sending one, and numbers can be masked once an email is handled.
  • Clean-up. Choose to delete old email text automatically after 30 days to 2 years, in line with the FTC Safeguards Rule's disposal expectation.
  • Audit log. Sends, deletions, exports and security changes are recorded on your computer, and you can download the log.
  • You're in control. Nothing is sent, archived or deleted without your click. Export all your data as a file, or delete your email history, from Settings.

What you should do too

  • Turn on disk encryption: BitLocker or Device Encryption on Windows, FileVault on macOS, LUKS on Linux.
  • Turn on C.A.P.'s app lock if anyone else uses the computer.
  • Use the local AI if you'd rather no email text ever leave your computer.

Your daily workflow

Here's how mail moves through C.A.P., and what's left for you to do:

  1. Mail arrivesC.A.P. checks your mailbox every 10 minutes (you can change this), or when you click Check for new mail. It reads mail without marking it as read.
  2. It's sortedEach email gets a quick look: needs a reply, or FYI (newsletters, receipts, notifications). Urgent mail, likely fraud and emails holding sensitive numbers are labelled.
  3. Replies are draftedUrgent mail first, using only your Knowledge. If you open an email that hasn't been drafted yet, it jumps the queue.
  4. You reviewRead the draft, check any amber facts, change the tone or wording, and add details.
  5. You decideSend it, snooze the email, turn it into a task, mark it Done, or delete it. Nothing happens without your click.
  6. C.A.P. follows upIf you ticked "Remind me if no answer", the email comes back on Today when they go quiet, with a one-click nudge.

A typical morning

  1. Open Today and read the headline: how many emails need you and who hasn't replied.
  2. Under Needs your reply, click Review draft on the first email. Urgent and fraud-flagged mail is at the top.
  3. Check the draft, then Send reply (or Ctrl + Enter). C.A.P. moves to the next email.
  4. Back on Today, use Draft a nudge for anyone under Waiting on others.
  5. Add any Suggested from email tasks you want to keep, and tick off what's Due today.
  6. Tax firms and agencies: glance at Key dates or Coming up for deadlines, missing documents and renewals.

Today

The headline tells you how many emails need you, how many people haven't replied, and the time C.A.P. saved you this week. Below it:

  • Needs your reply: the emails waiting on you, most important first, each with a one-line summary and a Review draft button. See all opens the Inbox.
  • Waiting on others: replies you sent with "Remind me if no answer" ticked that haven't been answered. Draft a nudge writes a polite follow-up for you to review; Dismiss stops tracking it.
  • Key dates (tax firms) or Coming up (insurance agencies): upcoming deadlines, clients still owing documents, certificates due this week and renewals in the next 60 days.
  • Due today: tasks due today; tick to complete.
  • Suggested from email: to-dos C.A.P. spotted in your mail. Click Add to put one on your task list.
  • Calendar: today's events, once Google Calendar is connected.

C.A.P. also shows how many of your recent drafts went out with little or no editing, so you can see how well it's learning.

Inbox

The list is on the left and the selected email is on the right. Use the filter box to find mail by sender or subject.

Reading an email

  • A short summary sits at the top; the full text is under Original message.
  • A fraud warning lists what looked wrong and what to do. Add a "verify by phone" task reminds you to call the sender on a number you already have before acting.
  • A sensitive information notice shows the number masked, reminds you not to repeat it in your reply, and suggests deleting the email once it's saved securely.
  • Tax firms and agencies see a green bar when the sender is a tracked client, with offers such as Mark received when they say they've sent documents, or Track this client's return for someone new.

After you reply

  • Send reply sends it from your own email account. Tick Remind me if no answer (ticked for you when your reply asks a question) to get a reminder on Today after 3 days, or the delay you set in Settings.
  • Snooze… hides the email until later today, tomorrow morning, next Monday or in a week.
  • Add task turns the email into a task linked back to it.
  • Done (E) files the email without replying and archives it in your mailbox.
  • Delete moves it to your mailbox's Trash. Click twice to confirm.

Working with a draft

Every draft is yours to edit. It's written from the email, your Knowledge and your signature, and it never invents prices, policies, dates or availability.

  • Quick replies: the buttons above the draft are short answers C.A.P. thinks fit, such as "Yes, Thursday works". Click one to rewrite the draft around it.
  • Tone: Professional, Friendly or Concise rewrites the draft in that tone. Set the default in Settings → You.
  • Insert saved reply… drops one of your saved replies in, filling in the client's first name, your name, your business name and your booking link. Check the details before sending.
  • Save as saved reply keeps a good draft for next time.
  • Tell the AI what to change: type an instruction such as "offer Thursday instead and mention the 10% repeat discount", then press Enter or click Rewrite. R jumps to this box.
  • Tasks from this email: to-dos the email asks of you, each with Add task.

Fact highlighting

Prices, dates, times, percentages, links and promises (refund, discount, free, guarantee) in the draft are highlighted as you type:

  • Green was found in the email, your business profile, files, saved replies or signature. The legend under the draft says where.
  • Amber isn't in any of them, so check it before sending. Click an amber item in the legend to jump to it.

Two safety checks when you send

  • Sensitive number in this reply: if your reply contains a Social Security, account or card number, C.A.P. stops and asks. Choose Edit reply to remove it (and point the client to your secure portal or a phone call), or Send anyway if you're sure.
  • Teach C.A.P. from your edit? If you changed a fact before sending, for example a price from $240 to $260, C.A.P. offers to update your business profile so future drafts get it right. If you rewrote most of the draft, it offers to save your version as a saved reply. Click Not now to skip.

Tasks

  • Type a task, optionally choose a due date and time and a priority (Normal, High or Low), and click Add task. High-priority tasks show in bold.
  • Tick Also add to calendar to put a task with a time on your Google Calendar (once it's connected).
  • Tasks made from an email have a from email link back to it.
  • Tick a task to complete it; Clear completed removes finished ones.
  • Next 7 days on the right shows your calendar for the week.

Clients (tax & bookkeeping)

Choose Tax & bookkeeping in Settings → Your industry and a Clients screen appears in the sidebar. It tracks each client's return and the documents they still owe you.

Adding a client

Click Add client, or open a client's email and choose Track this client's return. Fill in their name and email, status, an optional due date, the return type and tax year, and tick the documents they need to send. W-2, 1099-INT, 1098 mortgage interest, prior-year return and photo ID are ticked to start.

Statuses

Waiting on documents → In preparation → Ready to sign → Filed, or Extended. Change a status from the menu on the client's card. Use the tabs above the list to see open clients, one status, or all.

Documents

  • Click a document on the card to mark it received (click again to undo). The bar shows progress.
  • When a client emails to say they've sent documents, C.A.P. offers Mark received right in the email.
  • Once everything's in, the client moves to In preparation by itself.
  • Reminder for N missing documents writes an email listing exactly what's missing and pointing to your secure portal. Copy it or open it in your email app.

Key dates and timelines

  • Federal deadlines (estimated taxes, W-2s and 1099s, business and individual returns, extensions) are worked out every year and moved off weekends and holidays the way the IRS does.
  • Your dates: click Add date for state deadlines, office closures or anything else. Tick Repeats every year for yearly dates.
  • Client timeline: each card lists the return's due date and any dates you add for that client (choose the client under "For").
  • Dates in the next 120 days show under Key dates on this screen and on Today, with a countdown.

Policies (insurance)

Choose Insurance agency in Settings → Your industry and a Policies screen appears, with two lists.

  • Certificate requests: who asked, the certificate holder, whether they want to be listed as additional insured, and the due date. Statuses: Requested, Checking with carrier, Issued. When an email asks for a certificate, C.A.P. offers Track this certificate request and fills in the holder and due date it found.
  • Renewals: the client, policy, carrier, renewal date and premium change. Statuses: Upcoming, Re-shopping, Renewed, Lost. Check-in email writes a note to the client before renewal.

On Today, Coming up lists certificates due within a week and renewals within 60 days.

Knowledge

Knowledge is everything drafts are allowed to use. If a fact isn't here or in the email, the draft won't promise it. It has four tabs.

Business profile

Fill in as much as you can, then click Save profile:

  • Business name
  • What you do / who you serve
  • Services & prices
  • Hours, location, service area
  • Policies (booking, cancellation, refunds, payment)
  • Booking / payment / website links
  • Common questions & your answers
  • Never promise or say

"Never promise or say" is your safety list, for example "Never promise a refund amount" or "Don't confirm coverage before the carrier does". The industry starter kits fill it in for you if it's empty.

Saved replies

Answers you send often. Click New saved reply, give it a name and write the text. These placeholders are filled in when you insert it: {first_name}, {my_name}, {business_name} and {booking_link}. The AI also borrows their wording for similar emails. Industry starter kits adds 14 ready-made replies for tax firms or insurance agencies.

Files & notes

  • Upload files (or drag them in): price lists, FAQs, menus and policies as PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint or text, up to 25 MB each. The relevant passages are used in drafts.
  • New note for quick facts that don't fit the profile, like a parking note or a supplier's name.

Ask

Ask a question about your own business, such as "What's our cancellation policy for same-week bookings?". The answer comes only from your profile, files and notes, and lists its sources. It's a quick way to check what your drafts will say.

Security

The Security screen shows a score out of 100 based on how many protections are on, lists anything that Needs your attention (such as fraud attempts and emails holding Social Security numbers), and lets you turn each protection on.

Protections

  • Client email stays private from AI companies: on when drafting runs on your computer. With a cloud AI, tick Mask SSNs and account numbers before any cloud AI.
  • App lock: click Turn on app lock, choose a PIN (4 to 32 characters) and save the recovery code it shows somewhere safe, away from the computer. C.A.P. then asks for the PIN when it opens and after you've been away. Choose Lock after 5, 10, 15, 30 or 60 min away, Change PIN or Turn off. After 5 wrong PINs it waits before letting anyone try again.
  • Passwords and keys in the Windows vault: your email password and AI key are kept in Windows Credential Manager (macOS Keychain or the Linux keyring on those systems).
  • Old email text is disposed of: choose to keep email text until you delete it, or for 30 days, 90 days, 6 months, 1 year or 2 years after it's handled.
  • Numbers masked in handled emails: replaces full Social Security and account numbers in emails you've finished with.
  • Fraud & impersonation shield and Sensitive-number guard: always on; see Inbox.
  • Audit log: sends, deletions, exports and security changes are recorded. Download audit log saves it as a spreadsheet.
  • Disk encryption: C.A.P. can't check this itself. Turn on BitLocker or Device Encryption in Windows Settings, then tick to confirm.
  • Industry safety rules: on once you choose your industry.

Summary for your security plan writes a plain description of how C.A.P. handles client data, ready to paste into your written information security plan.

Settings

The top of Settings shows the folder where everything is stored on your computer.

  • Email account: connect or Disconnect your mailbox. See Connecting your email.
  • You: your name, default tone and signature (added to every draft exactly as written). Learn my style reads your recent sent mail (at least 3 emails) to match your usual greeting, sign-off and length.
  • Your industry: General small business, Tax & bookkeeping or Insurance agency. It changes what counts as urgent and what drafts must never promise, adds the Clients or Policies screen, and offers Add starter kit: 14 saved replies, safety rules and a document checklist. Nothing you've written is changed.
  • AI engine: On this computer (Ollama) or Cloud, with your API key. Pick a model and click Test and use this model. Extra quality check on each draft has the AI review its own work, which takes about twice as long on local models.
  • Google Calendar (optional): shows today's events and lets drafts avoid times you're already booked. Follow the steps shown to add your Google sign-in file, then Connect Google.
  • Inbox: how often to check for new mail (only when I click, or every 5, 10, 15, 30 or 60 minutes); when to write drafts (right after sorting, urgent first, or only when I open an email, fastest on slow computers); and after how many days to remind you about unanswered replies (1 to 14).
  • Sample inbox for demos: load, switch or remove a sample business.
  • Your data: Export my data (JSON) saves everything except passwords and keys; Summary for your security plan; and Delete email history removes stored email text from C.A.P. Your mailbox itself isn't touched.

Troubleshooting

A draft says "Draft failed"
Usually the AI engine wasn't reachable. Make sure Ollama is running (or your cloud key is valid in Settings → AI engine), then open the email and click Try again.
"Ollama isn't running"
Open the Ollama app, or run ollama serve, then click Test and use this model. If it says no models are installed, download one as shown in Choosing an AI engine.
Drafts are slow
Choose a smaller model, set Settings → Inbox → Write drafts to Only when I open an email, turn off the extra quality check, or use a cloud key.
An email that needs an answer went to FYI
Open it under the FYI tab and click Draft a reply anyway.
A draft got a fact wrong
Fix it before sending; C.A.P. offers to update your profile so it's right next time. Also check the fact is in Knowledge, and add anything you never want promised to "Never promise or say".
I forgot my PIN
On the lock screen, click Forgot your PIN? Use your recovery code and enter the code you saved. App lock turns off; set a new PIN in Security.
Email won't connect
Most providers need an app password, not your normal password, and two-step verification on to create one. Check the address and server names, then try again.
Where is my data?
On your computer only, in the folder shown at the top of Settings. Use Export my data for a copy.
