# Getting great drafts

C.A.P. drafts from four sources, in this order of trust:

1. **The email and its thread.** The latest message wins if it changes something said earlier.
2. **Knowledge → Business profile.** Your prices, hours, policies, links, FAQs and "never promise" list. Drafts only quote facts that appear here, in your files, or in the email.
3. **Knowledge → Saved replies.** Your own answers to common questions. When an incoming email is similar, the AI reuses their wording and facts.
4. **Your sent mail.** Settings → Learn my style picks up your usual greeting, sign-off and length.

## Saved replies worth creating first

| Name | What to include |
|---|---|
| Pricing | Your standard prices, what's included, how to book |
| Availability | How far ahead you book, typical lead time, booking link |
| Reschedule / cancel | Your policy and fee, how to pick a new time |
| Complaint | Apology, what you'll do (only what your guarantee covers), next step |
| Not a fit | A polite decline, plus a referral if you have one |

Placeholders: `{first_name}`, `{my_name}`, `{business_name}`, `{booking_link}`.

Remove private client details from saved replies. They are reused for other people.
