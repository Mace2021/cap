## Product name
C.A.P. Inbox Assistant

## Short description
Private AI email assistant for small offices. Sorts your inbox, flags fraud, and drafts replies from your own prices and policies, on your own PC.

## Description
C.A.P. reads your business email, works out what needs you, and drafts replies that quote your real prices, hours and policies. You read every draft and send it with one click. Nothing is ever sent without you.

It runs on your own computer. There is no C.A.P. account and no C.A.P. server, and your client email isn't sent to us. Use a free AI model on your PC through Ollama so email never leaves it, or connect your own OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, Groq or OpenRouter key.

BUILT FOR TAX & BOOKKEEPING FIRMS
• Tracks which documents each client still owes and writes the reminder email for you
• Federal tax deadlines worked out every year, plus your own state and client dates
• Flags look-alike IRS emails and warns before you send a Social Security number

BUILT FOR INSURANCE AGENCIES
• Spots certificate-of-insurance requests and their due dates
• Keeps policy renewals from slipping through the cracks
• Never confirms coverage before the carrier does

FOR ANY SMALL BUSINESS
• Answers routine questions from the facts you give it, and never invents prices or availability
• Every price, date and promise in a draft is highlighted: green when your information backs it, amber when you should check it
• Catches fake "new bank details" and payment-change emails before you reply

PRIVACY AND SECURITY
• App lock with a PIN, and passwords kept in Windows Credential Manager
• Automatic clean-up of old email text, and an audit log you can download
• No analytics or telemetry in the app

Try it first with a built-in sample inbox (home cleaning, tax firm or insurance agency) before connecting your own email. Works with Gmail and Google Workspace, Yahoo, iCloud, AOL, Zoho, Fastmail and your own business domain. Outlook and Microsoft 365 sign-in is coming in a later release.

Setup guide, privacy details and feedback: https://mace2021.github.io/cap/

## Additional system requirements
64-bit Windows 10 (version 1809) or Windows 11. 8 GB of memory minimum; 16 GB recommended to run the AI on your own PC. About 1.5 GB of disk space, plus 3 to 15 GB for an optional local AI model. Internet connection needed for email and for cloud AI.
